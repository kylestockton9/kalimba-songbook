For a 17-key kalimba tuned in C.

Songbook

Pick a tune and the key to play next lights up. Tap it on screen, or tap Follow me and the app uses the microphone to keep up as you pluck your own kalimba. Hear song plays the tune for you.

Tuner

Start the tuner, then pluck one key at a time and let it ring. Tap a key below to hear the right note and check only that key.

The tuner needs the microphone, which this page is not allowed to use. Open the web version in Chrome: kylestockton9.github.io/kalimba-songbook
